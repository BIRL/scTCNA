One noise metric for a list of genes across all cancer types. Cells are blank where a gene is absent from a dataset. Values are not batch-corrected across studies; the row z-score option rescales each gene over the cancer types it is measured in.

Rows are genes, columns cancer types; the CSV also holds the q-values when the metric has a paired test.

scTCNA is a single-cell RNA-seq counterpart of The Cancer Noise Atlas. If you use it, please cite: Shah R, Faisal A, et al., Chaudhary SU. The Cancer Noise Atlas (TCNA): A Webserver of Multiscale Transcriptional Noise in Cancer. J Mol Biol 2026. tcna.lums.edu.pk

Data: 3CA (Gavish et al., Nature 2023). Full methods and references on the About page.
