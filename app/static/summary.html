Cross-cancer summary

Is transcriptional noise higher in malignant cells than in non-malignant cells of the same patients? For every patient, genes detected in at least 10% of cells of both groups are compared and the median over genes is taken; patients are then tested against zero with a Wilcoxon signed-rank test, so the patient is the unit of replication. Three measures are shown because they answer different questions: the raw CV ratio (TCNA Eq. 1), the same ratio after downsampling all cells to a common UMI depth, and the depth-matched excess noise, i.e. how far malignant genes lie above the reference cells' mean-CV² trend at the same expression level.
