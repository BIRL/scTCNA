Tumor Heterogeneity

DEPTH2 and DEPTH scores on log2-transformed expression, as in TCNA. At the cell level every cell is a sample: DEPTH2 z-scores genes across the cells of the same patient and cell group (how atypical a cell is within its own tumour), and DEPTH scores malignant cells against the mean of the non-malignant cells (its "normal" reference, so non-malignant cells have no DEPTH). At the sample level each patient x cell group pseudo-bulk profile is a sample. DEPTH2 is compared between malignant and non-malignant cells with a paired Wilcoxon test across patients.
