Tumor Heterogeneity

DEPTH2 and DEPTH scores on log2-transformed expression, as in TCNA. At the cell level every cell is a sample (genes z-scored across all cells of the dataset); at the sample level each patient x compartment pseudo-bulk profile is a sample. Compartments are compared with a paired Wilcoxon test across patients.
