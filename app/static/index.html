Gene Noise Analysis

Transcriptional noise of a gene in malignant cells versus non-malignant cells of the same patients. Values are medians across patients of per-patient metrics on CP10K-normalised counts; differential noise is log2(CVmal/CVnon-mal) per patient (TCNA Eq. 1), tested with a paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test.
